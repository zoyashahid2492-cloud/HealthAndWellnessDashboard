# Healthy Living Website — Phase 1 & Phase 2 Product Direction

## Objective

Instead of presenting **Variant 1** and **Variant 2** as alternative directions, we want to combine the approved ideas into **one product experience delivered in two phases**.

The overall vision remains the same. The difference between Phase 1 and Phase 2 is based on **technical complexity, integration requirements, and time to launch**.

A key principle is that the Healthy Living website should **complement Sahatna rather than duplicate it**.

---

# Core Product Principle

### Healthy Living Website

The website should focus on:

* Discovery
* Education
* Research
* Engagement
* Lightweight AI-generated wellness tools
* Schools and family initiatives
* Policy and global leadership
* Collaboration

### Sahatna

Sahatna should remain the primary platform for:

* Personal health management
* Persistent user health data
* Tracking
* Monitoring
* Scanning
* Health records
* Device integrations
* Advanced personalization

Where a feature belongs more naturally within Sahatna, the website should **redirect or connect the user to Sahatna instead of recreating the experience**.

---

# Navigation Direction

Suggested main website structure:

* Home
* Healthy Living
* Schools & Children
* Global & Research
* Tools
* Collaborate With Us
* Sahatna / Sign In

We do **not necessarily need “Connect” as a standalone navigation item**.

Instead, Sahatna should be integrated throughout the website through contextual CTAs such as:

* Sign in with Sahatna
* Continue in Sahatna
* Open in Sahatna
* Track this in Sahatna

---

# PHASE 1

## Goal

Launch the strongest version of the website using features that require the **least integrations and lowest technical complexity**, while still making the platform useful, interactive and differentiated.

Phase 1 should feel complete on its own.

---

## 1. Home

The homepage should introduce Healthy Living as an ecosystem connecting:

* Individuals
* Families
* Schools
* Research
* Policy
* Community initiatives

The homepage should guide different audiences into the most relevant areas of the platform.

Suggested CTAs:

* Explore Healthy Living
* Create a Wellness Plan
* Explore Schools & Children
* Explore Research
* Collaborate With Us
* Open Sahatna

---

## 2. Lightweight Wellness Tools

Users should be able to interact with useful wellness tools directly on the website without requiring complex health integrations.

### Meal Plan Generator

Users provide simple inputs such as:

* Goal
* Dietary preference
* Number of meals
* Lifestyle
* Basic preferences

The website generates a suggested meal plan.

The experience should remain lightweight and educational rather than becoming a full nutrition-tracking platform.

CTA after generation:

**Track or personalize further in Sahatna**

---

### Workout / Gym Plan Generator

Users provide inputs such as:

* Fitness goal
* Experience level
* Available time
* Training location
* Number of days per week

The website generates a simple workout plan.

Possible outputs:

* Weekly schedule
* Exercises
* Sets / reps
* Basic guidance

CTA:

**Continue your fitness journey in Sahatna**

---

### Basic Wellness Recommendations

Users can answer a short set of questions and receive general recommendations around areas such as:

* Movement
* Nutrition
* Sleep
* Wellbeing
* Healthy habits

This should not require medical records or detailed personal health integrations.

---

# 3. Sahatna Integration — Phase 1

Avoid duplicating Sahatna functionality.

Where appropriate, provide:

### Sahatna CTAs

* Sign in with Sahatna
* Open Sahatna
* Continue in Sahatna

If Single Sign-On or Sahatna authentication is technically simple enough, it can be included in Phase 1.

If it requires significant backend work, Phase 1 can initially use a clear redirect into Sahatna.

The website should not create a separate competing personal-health account ecosystem.

---

# 4. Global & Research

This section should communicate leadership in:

* Healthy living
* Public health
* Policy
* Research
* Evidence-based initiatives

The positioning should communicate ambition and global leadership without making unsupported ranking claims.

Suggested positioning:

**Setting a global benchmark for progressive health and wellbeing policy.**

Phase 1 can include:

* Research publications
* Reports
* Academic journals
* Insights

Possible categories:

* Nutrition

* Research updates

# 5. Collaborate With Us

Possible audiences:

* Universities

* Children

# PHASE 2

## Goal

Phase 2 introduces capabilities that require deeper integrations, persistent user data, computer vision, health information or Sahatna APIs.

These features should build on the Phase 1 experience rather than replace it.

---

# 1. Advanced Sahatna Integration

Phase 2 can introduce deeper integration between Healthy Living and Sahatna.

Potential capabilities:

* Sahatna Single Sign-On
* User profile synchronization
* Saved plans
* Personalized recommendations
* Progress tracking
* Health history
* Cross-platform experiences

The website should still avoid becoming a duplicate of Sahatna.

Advanced health-management actions should continue inside Sahatna where appropriate.

---

# 2. Scanning Experiences

Scanning features should be moved to Phase 2 because of their technical and integration complexity.

Potential experiences:

### Food Scan

Users scan or photograph food to receive information such as:

* Food identification
* Nutrition information
* Suggested alternatives
* Health guidance

### Barcode Scan

Scan packaged foods for:

* Nutrition information
* Ingredient information
* Health indicators
* Recommended alternatives

### Other Health Scanning Features

Any scanning experience requiring:

* Camera access
* Computer vision
* AI recognition
* Health databases
* Sahatna data

should be considered Phase 2.

---

# 3. Advanced Meal Personalization

Phase 2 can improve the meal-plan generator using Sahatna data such as:

* Personal goals
* Existing health profile
* Preferences
* Activity
* Progress
* Previous plans

Users could save plans directly into Sahatna.

---

# 4. Advanced Fitness Personalization

Workout recommendations can become more personalized using:

* User profile
* Activity history
* Fitness goals
* Progress
* Available equipment
* Previous plans

Users should be able to:

* Save plans
* Track completion
* View progress
* Continue inside Sahatna

---

# 5. Tracking & Dashboards

Phase 2 can introduce authenticated dashboards where appropriate.

Potential areas:

* Personal progress
* Saved plans
* Participation
* School initiatives
* Program outcomes
* Research engagement

Any health-specific personal dashboard should be connected to Sahatna rather than built as a separate competing system.

---

# 6. Schools Data Expansion

As more data becomes available, Schools & Children can evolve into a more detailed insights platform.

Potential Phase 2 dashboards:

* Student participation
* School participation
* Program reach
* Family engagement
* Behavioral indicators
* Trends over time
* Program effectiveness
* Geographic comparison
* Initiative impact

---

# 7. Research Platform Expansion

The research platform can evolve into a more advanced academic ecosystem.

Potential Phase 2 functionality:

* User accounts
* Premium subscriptions
* Paid research access
* Academic contributor profiles
* Research submissions
* University partnerships
* Research collaboration spaces
* Saved publications
* Personalized research feeds
* Advanced search and filtering

---

# Phase Definition

## Phase 1

**Launch quickly with high-value, low-integration experiences.**

Includes:

* Core Healthy Living website
* Meal plan generator
* Workout plan generator
* Lightweight wellness recommendations
* Sahatna redirects / basic authentication where feasible
* Global & Research
* Research publications
* Research subscription / registration
* Schools & Children
* Influence & mobilization storytelling
* Collaboration
* CSAT
* Comments

---

## Phase 2

**Add experiences requiring deeper technology and integrations.**

Includes:

* Scanning
* Advanced Sahatna integration
* Personal health data
* Saved plans
* Progress tracking
* Advanced personalization
* Personal dashboards
* Expanded school analytics
* Advanced research platform
* Paid research subscriptions
* Device / health integrations

---

# Design Direction for Figma

Do not design Phase 1 and Phase 2 as two different visual variants.

Create **one unified design system and one complete product vision**.

Use the existing preferred concepts from both previous variants.

Pages and components should be annotated as:

**Phase 1**

or

**Phase 2**

Where a Phase 2 feature appears within a Phase 1 journey, it can be represented as:

* Coming Soon
* Continue in Sahatna
* Future Capability

The overall design should demonstrate the **full long-term vision**, while clearly identifying which functionality is intended for the initial launch.

---

# Simple Product Rule

When deciding whether a feature belongs in Phase 1 or Phase 2:

### Phase 1

Can we deliver this using:

**Content + simple user inputs + lightweight AI + basic forms?**

If yes, it should generally be Phase 1.

### Phase 2

Does it require:

**Health data + scanning + persistent tracking + device integrations + advanced Sahatna APIs + significant backend development?**

If yes, it should generally be Phase 2.

---

# Overall Experience

The relationship between the two platforms should be clear:

**Healthy Living**

Discover → Learn → Generate → Participate → Collaborate

**Sahatna**

Personalize → Track → Monitor → Manage
