# Healthy Living Abu Dhabi — Figma AI Prompt Pack
Two full website revamps, each as one connected multi-page site.

**How to use this:** Use the two **MASTER PROMPTS** below first — each one asks Figma AI to generate a full multi-page website in one shot, including the original site's core pages (Our Approach, Latest Updates, About Us) alongside the new feature pages, so it comes back as one connected site instead of a single isolated screen. If Figma AI only returns one frame at a time, paste the master prompt once, then say "now generate the next page: [page name]" to keep it inside the same file/style instead of starting a fresh unrelated screen. The **per-screen prompts** further down are for refining or regenerating one page at a time once the site structure exists.

---

## MASTER PROMPT — Variant A: "Connected & Rewarded"
*(Paste this as one single prompt)*

```
Design a full multi-page website revamp for "Healthy Living," Abu Dhabi's government-led 
public health program, called the "Connected & Rewarded" variant. This must be one 
connected website with a consistent nav, footer, and visual system across every page — 
not a single standalone screen.

BRAND & CONTENT (this is a real government program — keep this content on every relevant 
page, don't drop it):
- Tagline: "Making healthy living the easy choice for all"
- Mission: "Healthy Living is Abu Dhabi's government-led program empowering every citizen 
  and resident to live longer, healthier, more fulfilling lives."
- Three core priorities: "Active Lifestyles", "Healthy Eating", "Prevention-First"
- Real initiatives to reference: "Degayeg Initiative", "Festival of Health", "Nutri-Mark 
  Label", "OOH Healthy Policy"
- The site currently has 3 core pages that MUST be retained and redesigned, not removed: 
  "Our Approach" (how they work across government, private sector, communities and 
  individuals to shape systems, policy and infrastructure), "Latest Updates" (a news/
  press feed), and "About Us" (who they are, mission, milestones)

VISUAL DIRECTION (this must match the real Healthy Living brand exactly — do not invent a 
new color scheme):
- Warm cream/blush background #FBF1EC as the main content background, with dusty rose 
  #F3DDD8 used for contrast card panels within sections
- Vivid grass green #1FA355 as the primary action color — used on the active/selected 
  nav pill, primary CTA buttons, and small accent icons/badges. This is NOT a background 
  wash color, it's a bold accent used sparingly and intentionally
- Coral-orange #F0603C used only as small accents: underline squiggles beneath 
  subheadings, small icon fills, occasional badge tags
- Navy blue #1E3A8A used as a secondary accent for numbered step indicators, arrows, and 
  occasional badge tags
- Black #0A0A0A for the footer background (white text/logo on black), and for bold 
  display headlines on light backgrounds
- Full-bleed real photography hero images on every page (Emirati families, joggers, 
  community life along Abu Dhabi's corniche and parks) with a large-radius white/cream 
  rounded content panel overlapping the bottom of the hero image
- Bold, chunky geometric/grotesk display font for headlines (like the real site's 
  rounded-bold wordmark style), clean simple sans for body copy, bilingual-ready with 
  the logo lockup stacked as "HEALTHY LIVING" in English above the Arabic wordmark
- Pill-shaped nav buttons in the top nav, with the active page shown as a solid green 
  filled pill and inactive pages as white outlined pills
- Breadcrumb trail under the hero on inner pages (e.g. "Home → About us")
- News/content card tags use rotating accent colors (green, blue, coral/pink) as small 
  pill labels, not one fixed color
- Persistent floating "Ask Healthy Living AI" chat bubble bottom-right on every page, 
  styled in the same green/cream/black palette

SITEMAP — generate all of these as one connected site, same nav/footer on each:
1. HOME — hero with tagline + "Connect Your Wearable" and "Explore Our Approach" CTAs, 
   "why connect your device" 3-card row, the 3 priorities cards, a "Latest Updates" news 
   preview strip (3 cards), the 4 initiative pill links, a community stats strip, footer
2. OUR APPROACH — intro paragraph on shaping systems/policy/infrastructure across 
   government, private sector, communities, individuals; accordion or 3-column section 
   for the 3 priorities each with description; a simple 4-step "how we work" process 
   diagram (Understand → Design → Partner → Measure); closing CTA band
3. LATEST UPDATES — full news feed page: filter chips by category, list of news cards 
   with date/title/excerpt/tag, pagination, a featured/pinned story at top
4. ABOUT US — mission statement, "who we are" section, partner/government logo strip, 
   milestone timeline (program launch, Festival of Health 2025, strategy publication, 25 
   strategic initiatives rollout)
5. CONNECT YOUR WEARABLE — device connection cards (Apple Health, Google Fit, Fitbit, 
   Samsung Health) with connect buttons, a data-privacy reassurance panel, then a 
   connected-state progress summary (steps ring, active minutes, sleep hours) and a 
   7-day trend chart
6. REWARDS — tier/points summary bar, "ways to earn" 6-card grid, "redeem your points" 
   partner reward carousel, an opt-in anonymized community leaderboard
7. POLICY DOCUMENTS — searchable/filterable document library with title, file type, 
   size, date, and download button per row; a "recently added" highlight; a prompt 
   linking to the AI assistant for help finding documents
8. OUR PARTNERS — a page featuring the organizations supporting Healthy Living: a hero 
   intro ("Working together for a healthier Abu Dhabi"), grouped partner logo grids by 
   category (Government Entities, Healthcare Providers, Private Sector, Community 
   Organizations), each logo tile with name + one-line description of their role, and a 
   "Become a Partner" CTA card with a contact/enquiry button
9. PRESS — a media/press page separate from the general news feed: press release list 
   (title, date, download PDF), a "Media Kit" card (logo pack, brand guidelines, official 
   photos — downloadable zip), and a "Media Contact" card with a contact form for 
   journalist enquiries
10. FAQ — an accordion-style FAQ page grouped by category (About the Program, Connecting 
    Your Device, Rewards & Points, Privacy & Data, Policies): each category as a 
    collapsible section with 4-6 expandable question/answer rows; a search bar at top to 
    filter questions; a closing "Still have questions? Ask our AI" callout linking to the 
    assistant
11. ASK HEALTHY LIVING (AI) — full assistant/discovery page: central ask bar with prompt 
    text "Ask anything, or discover something new", topic shortcut cards (Policies, 
    Rewards, Nutrition, Events, Initiatives, Device Data, Partners, FAQs), a "Discover 
    more" module below the chat suggesting related content the user hasn't seen yet 
    (e.g. "You might also like: Festival of Health", "New policy: Nutri-Mark Label 
    update"), a sample realistic Q&A thread, and a disclaimer strip: "This assistant 
    provides general guidance and is not a substitute for medical advice."

Generate Home first to establish the shared nav, footer, buttons and card components, 
then generate each remaining page reusing those exact same components and colors.
```
